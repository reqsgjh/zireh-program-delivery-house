Founder Bio

Prabhu Joseph

Founder and Principal

Prabhu built Zireh around a simple observation: complex programs rarely fail because nobody knows the methodology. They fail when the real position becomes hard to see, accountability blurs, decisions slow down and confidence disappears.

His approach is practical and direct. He works across executives, delivery teams and vendors to understand what is actually happening, establish a workable baseline and put the right governance, people and cadence around the program.

Prabhu has experience across telcos, data and reporting, ERP, networks, finance transformation and enterprise technology delivery.

Through Zireh, he combines his own senior program leadership with a network of experienced operators and specialists who can be brought into the work when needed.

Our principle

Trust • Honesty • Control

Truth, no theatre. Direct, human and evidence-led. Never faceless, inflated or capability-heavy.
